# Lid Up Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Lid Up is a menu bar utility for macOS that plays a folding animation on the
built-in display while the MacBook lid closes.

## What the app accesses

- **Lid hinge angle.** Read from the MacBook's built-in hinge sensor through
  the macOS HID interface. Used only to drive the animation.
- **Screen content.** With your Screen Recording permission, the app captures
  the built-in display only while the lid is moving, so it can draw a live copy
  of your desktop inside the animation. Frames are held in graphics memory for
  the duration of the fold and are discarded when it ends.

## What the app does not do

- It does not store screen recordings, screenshots or sensor readings.
- It does not send any data anywhere. The app has no network features, no
  accounts, no analytics and no advertising.
- It does not collect personal information of any kind.

## Settings

Your preferences (angles, blur, launch at login and similar) are stored
locally in the app's own preferences on your Mac.
